# Refunds

This demo knowledge base does not define refund eligibility, timing, payment method, or bank
processing time. The only defined refund calculation is the mock CRM cancellation result: it deducts
a 10% cancellation fee and returns the remaining amount as `refund_amount`. Do not infer other
refund rules from that calculation. Escalate questions about refund timing, status, exceptions, or
eligibility to a human support agent.

# GST, taxes, and fees

The mock CRM's only configured booking charge is its 10% cancellation fee, calculated from the
booking amount. This demo knowledge base does not define GST rates, whether a price includes GST,
tax invoices, convenience fees, payment fees, or other service charges. Do not infer tax treatment
or additional fees. Escalate GST, invoice, and fee questions beyond the stated cancellation
calculation to a human support agent.
